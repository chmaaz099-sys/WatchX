Help & Support Center

Frequently Asked Questions & Technical Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I stream in 1080p Full HD?
All released titles on WatchX are served in 1080p FHD by default. If your network speed fluctuates, click the Server button in the video player to switch seamlessly between Server 1, Server 2, Server 3, Server 4, and Server 5.
How do I set up a 4-Digit Profile PIN?
Navigate to Settings > Profile > 4-Digit Security PIN. Enter your desired 4-digit code. WatchX securely hashes your PIN using PBKDF2 cryptography with unique random salts.
How does the 240Hz Extreme Performance Engine work?
Press Shift+F on your keyboard to reveal the real-time frame rate HUD. If you have a 120Hz, 144Hz, 165Hz, or 240Hz monitor, WatchX synchronizes all carousels, modals, and animations to your display's native refresh rate using requestAnimationFrame.
How does Coming Soon Notify Me work?
On the Coming Soon page, click the 'Notify Me' button on any upcoming movie or series. WatchX adds the release to your personal notifications center and notifies you the day the title becomes streamable.
